What the tool adds
Your SAR file stays as it is; the tool appends:
  1. PPortal — days attendance is marked on the portal:
    =COUNTIF(Attendance!Student Id column, Student Id)
  2. DDiff — Class Attendance − Portal. Anything other than 0 means the portal is behind (or ahead of) class.
  3. AThe processed attendance is copied into an Attendance sheet inside the SAR workbook, so the formulas stay live in Excel.
